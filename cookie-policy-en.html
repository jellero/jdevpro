Cookie Policy

How JDEVPRO handles necessary storage and Google Analytics.

Your choice

Google Analytics is not loaded until you explicitly choose “Accept analytics”. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the website. Google advertising features always remain denied.

Necessary preference storage

Your choice is stored in browser localStorage under jdevpro_cookie_consent_v1. It is used only to remember whether you accepted or rejected Analytics and is not sent to third parties.

Google Analytics 4

After consent, Google Analytics 4 is loaded with measurement ID G-K33R7FQ50C. Analytics may collect visit and session statistics, approximate location, and browser/device information.

First-party Analytics cookies may include _ga and _ga_<container-id>, normally with a maximum lifetime of 2 years. Google Ads, remarketing and advertising personalisation are not configured.

Withdraw or change consent

You can reopen the panel at any time using “Cookie preferences”. If you reject after previously accepting, the website updates analytics consent to “denied” and attempts to remove first-party Analytics cookies from the JDEVPRO domain.

More information

See the Privacy Policy. Contact: info@jdevpro.it.
