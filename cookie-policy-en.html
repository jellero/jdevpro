Cookie policy

Cookies: only what is needed, statistics if you want.

Without your consent the site uses no cookies: it only saves your choice in the browser. Google Analytics is enabled only if you accept it.

Your choice

On your first visit you can accept or reject statistics with two equal buttons. Until you choose, Google Analytics is not loaded. Rejecting does not limit your use of the site. Google advertising features are always off: no remarketing, no personalised ads.

What we store in your browser

  • jdevpro_cookie_consent_v1 (local storage, technical): remembers whether you accepted or rejected statistics. It stays in the browser until you delete it and is never sent to anyone. It needs no consent;
  • _ga (Google Analytics cookie, statistics): tells visitors apart anonymously. Lasts 2 years. Only with your consent;
  • _ga_K33R7FQ50C (Google Analytics cookie, statistics): keeps session state. Lasts 2 years. Only with your consent.

Google Analytics 4

The service is provided by Google Ireland Limited. It collects aggregated statistics on visits and page views, approximate location, device type and browser; Google Analytics 4 does not log IP addresses. More in Google's privacy policy ↗ and on how Google uses data from partner sites ↗.

No other external services

The site does not load fonts, maps, videos, social buttons or other content from external services. Links to WhatsApp, LinkedIn or GitHub open only if you click them.

Changing your mind

You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie preferences” button below or at the bottom right of every page. If you withdraw consent, the site switches off Google Analytics and deletes its cookies. You can also clear cookies and site data from your browser settings.

For all other processing see the privacy notice. Questions: info@jdevpro.it.
