JDEVPRO · Technical support

Technical support: one place for requests about deployed systems.

For software, industrial machine integrations and connected systems handled by JDEVPRO, support scope, priorities and intervention arrangements depend on the agreed service.

Prepare a support request

This form prepares an email in your email application. It does not automatically create a ticket or send data through the website. You must send the message yourself.

No automatic submission. Support scope and any guaranteed response times must be agreed in writing.

Support

How requests are handled

The impact category describes the issue; it does not establish a guaranteed priority. For critical systems, only the active support agreement determines commitments.

01 · IdentifyProvide company, system and project reference.
02 · DescribeExplain the symptom, when it began and how operations are affected.
03 · RespondResponse arrangements follow the agreed support terms.
04 · RecordDocument work and outstanding actions within the project's scope.

Support

Maintenance and service levels

Maintenance plans can define the following terms in writing, without implying that they apply to every project:

  • Reporting channels and support hours
  • Initial response times and escalation rules
  • Periodic checks, updates and backups
  • Remote, on-site and recovery arrangements

Support

Security and remote access

Remote access is authorised for each project using individual accounts, limited permissions and revocable access. VPN, MFA and access logs are used where available and appropriate. Never send passwords or sensitive data via this form.
