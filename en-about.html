How we work: from machines to business software, with a clear process.

We develop integrations between industrial machines, PLCs, CNCs and business software. We assess interfaces, build data exchange, test the connections and prepare technical documentation for the relevant procedures. Scope and responsibilities are agreed before work begins.

Our process

From assessment to documentation, with clearly defined steps.

Each project starts with the machinery and systems already in place. The goal is verifiable data exchange, not just a network connection.

  1. Assess the interfacesIdentify the machine make and model, PLC or CNC, available protocols, company network and business software. Define data exchange and project scope.
  2. Develop the integrationBuild the software, gateways and machine-to-business-system connections using the available interfaces and respecting plant operating conditions.
  3. Test with real production dataVerify the exchange of jobs, machine states, progress and alarms according to the functions supported by the controller.
  4. Document the resultDocument configurations, connections, exchanged data and test evidence. Independent assessors remain responsible for certification and eligibility decisions.

Accountability

A named technical contact and clear professional responsibilities.

JDEVPRO is the professional business of Jacopo Ellero, who handles software development and technical integration between machines, infrastructure and business systems. Customers have an identifiable technical point of contact.

Where electrical wiring or panels are required, those activities are performed by licensed installers. Independent assessors remain responsible for tax-incentive evaluations and certification: developing the interconnection and assessing it are separate responsibilities.
