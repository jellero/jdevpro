Home Assistant · Hotels · Restaurants

Home Assistant automation: designed to last.

Home Assistant connects almost anything. The hard part is keeping it running for years, in a property where different people work and guests must not notice a thing. We design the system, choose devices that do not depend on a manufacturer's cloud, install it and document it.

The problem

Sound familiar?

  • Automation built from different apps. Each brand has its own app and cloud: when one shuts down or changes, part of the system stops working.
  • Without internet everything stops. Cloud-based thermostats, lights and sensors fail exactly when you need them.
  • One update and something breaks. Hand-made configurations with no backups or documentation that only their author can fix.
  • Home systems used in a hotel. Staff have no overview and guests find the controls confusing.

FAQ

Answers before you ask.

Is Home Assistant suitable for professional use?

Yes, if it is designed as a proper system: dedicated hardware, separate network, devices chosen to last, updates tested before installing, backups and documentation. That is what we do.

What if the internet goes down?

Automations run on site and keep working. Remote access waits for the connection, but lights, climate and local alarms stay active.

Can I keep my existing devices?

Often yes. We assess them during the survey: those depending on an unreliable cloud are flagged and, if needed, we suggest compatible alternatives.

Is there a subscription?

No. Home Assistant has no licences. Updates and support can be handled by us with a monthly plan or on call.

Does the temperature log work for HACCP?

We record and store cold-room and fridge temperatures with alerts on anomalies. The HACCP plan procedures remain the responsibility of the manager and their consultant.

Do you do the wiring?

Wiring, lines and electrical panels are installed by licensed electricians under Italian law (DM 37/08), whom we work with. We take care of devices, configuration, software and data.
