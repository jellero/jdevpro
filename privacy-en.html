Notice under Articles 13 and 14 GDPR

Privacy: what data we process and why.

The site has no forms, accounts or profiling. We process the data you send when you contact us and, only with your consent, anonymous visit statistics.

Data controller

Jacopo Ellero, owner of the JDEVPRO business, Tolmezzo (UD), Italy, VAT IT02783420306. For any request about personal data: info@jdevpro.it.

What data we process

  • Data you send us: name, contact details and message content when you write by email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn;
  • Technical browsing data: the server hosting the site automatically logs IP address, date and time, requested page and browser, to run and protect it;
  • Visit statistics: only if you accept, through Google Analytics 4, as described in the cookie policy.

Why we process it

  • to answer your requests and prepare quotes: pre-contractual steps you asked for (Art. 6.1.b GDPR);
  • to carry out the agreed work and meet tax and accounting obligations: contract and legal obligations (Art. 6.1.b and 6.1.c);
  • to keep the site secure and working: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f);
  • to measure visits: consent (Art. 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time.

Do you have to provide it?

No. Without your contact details, however, we cannot reply. Rejecting statistics does not limit your use of the site in any way.

Who receives it

Data is processed by us and, only as far as needed, by providers acting as processors: website hosting, email service, tax adviser and, if you consented, Google for statistics. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

If you write to us on WhatsApp or LinkedIn, the message also passes through Meta or LinkedIn, which process it under their own policies.

Transfers outside the European Union

Google, Meta and LinkedIn may process data in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US adequacy decision (Data Privacy Framework) or on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

How long we keep it

  • requests with no follow-up: as long as needed to reply and at most 24 months;
  • customer data and accounting records: 10 years, as required by Article 2220 of the Italian Civil Code;
  • technical browsing data: for the period set by the hosting provider for service security;
  • Google Analytics statistics: for the period set in the service, at most 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data, restrict processing, receive it in a readable format or object to processing based on legitimate interest (Articles 15-22 GDPR). You can withdraw consent to statistics from the “Cookie preferences” button. Write to info@jdevpro.it: we reply within one month.

If you believe the processing is not correct you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante) ↗.

Automated decisions and external links

We make no automated decisions and do no profiling. The site links to external services such as GitHub, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, welora.it and akios.cloud: when you open them, their own policies apply.
