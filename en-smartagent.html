SmartAgent: less time in the office, more time with clients.

SmartAgent brings clients, email, appointments, route planning, cases, quotes, invoices, documents and expenses into one app that keeps itself up to date. Connect the email, calendar and contacts you already use: emails land in the right client record, your day is planned on a map, and finished work becomes a quote, an invoice and a mileage claim without retyping. Coming soon, SmartAgent Drive: storage and backup for your PC files, already sorted by client. The app is in Italian.

Days on the road

The dashboard

Open it in the morning and you already know what to do.

What it includes

Everything you use every day, connected.

Clients and email

Every email, including Italian certified email (PEC), goes to the client record by itself; client attachments go to their documents.

Calendar and route

Your day on a map, travel times with expected traffic, a "leave now" alert and a ready-made late message.

Cases and deadlines

Deals, assignments, projects or files with phases, checklists, hours and reminders.

Quotes and invoices

Quotes accepted online by the client, invoices with deposits and balances, Fatture in Cloud integration.

Documents

Versions, text read even from scans and full-text search; documents are matched to the right client.

Notes and dictation

Write or dictate two lines after a visit: the note finds the client by itself.

Mileage and expenses

Mileage from the day's route, receipts read from a photo.

Client portal

A private link for each client: job status, appointments, quotes, invoices and documents.

Teams

Several users, tasks and cases handed over between colleagues, private cases, each person's calendar on their phone.

SmartAgent Drive · coming soon

Storage and backup for your PC files, already sorted by client.

  1. Create the clientTheir folder appears on your PC or Mac, in Finder and File Explorer, with a folder for each case.
  2. Save a fileIt goes straight to the server and into the client record, for you and the colleagues who follow them.
  3. An email arrivesThe client's attachment lands in their folder by itself, right on your computer.
  4. Upload from your phoneA document uploaded to the record shows up in the folder on your computer, with nothing to download.
  5. Work as usualOpen files with AutoCAD, Word or Excel: save, and the new version goes into the case.

Backup and security

Your files safe, even with the PC switched off.

Drive is storage and backup in one: every file is on the server and in the app, reachable from your phone or another computer even when yours is off.

Continuous backup

Every file saved in the folders is copied to the server while you work. No USB sticks or external drives to remember.

History and recovery

Every save is a version: go back to yesterday's or last month's whenever you want.

Viruses and ransomware

If the PC is hit, previous versions stay on the server and the computer cannot delete them.

Broken, lost or stolen PC

Disconnect it from your profile and find everything on the new computer. Meanwhile your files are in the app, on your phone too.

The day

Today's clients in order on the map, with the time to leave.

The flow

From first contact to payment, without retyping.

  1. ContactThe client's email lands in their record; your phone's contacts know who is calling.
  2. AppointmentBooked by you or by the client online, with travel time and a departure alert.
  3. VisitOutcome and next step written or dictated right away; photos and signed report on the phone.
  4. QuotePrepared from the case, accepted by the client through a link.
  5. WorkPhases, hours, mileage and documents in the case; automations for repetitive steps.
  6. Invoice and paymentInvoice from the case or the quote, deposits and balances, reminder when overdue.

Works with what you use

No need to change habits.

  • Email and certified email
  • Two-way calendar
  • Phone contacts
  • APIs and webhooks

SmartAgent connects to Gmail, Microsoft 365, Aruba, certified email and any IMAP mailbox; to Google, Microsoft and iCloud calendars; to iPhone and Android contacts. Emails stay in your mailbox and appointments sync both ways.

It runs in the browser on computer, tablet and phone, with nothing to install. APIs and webhooks connect it to your own software or website.
