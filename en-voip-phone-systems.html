VoIP · Phone systems · Intercoms

VoIP phone systems: the office phone, anywhere.

We set up cloud or on-site VoIP phone systems: extensions on mobile and PC, auto attendant, opening hours and queues, intercoms ringing on your phone and calls connected to your business software.

Let's talk

The problem

Sound familiar?

  • Missed calls away from the office. If you are not at your desk, customers hear the phone ring out.
  • The old phone system is costly and fixed. Lines, maintenance and desk phones tied to one site.
  • You don't know who is calling. Every time you look the customer up by hand.

What you get

What we do, in practice.

Cloud or on-site

No hardware to buy, or a phone system on your premises if you prefer.

Extensions anywhere

Your office extension on mobile and PC, from home or on the road.

Auto attendant and hours

Greetings, routing by department, out-of-hours and queues.

Intercom on your phone

SIP intercoms calling extensions and mobiles, with door opening.

Connected to business software

Customer record on incoming calls, click to call and call history.

Numbers and lines

Keep your existing numbers and use VoIP carrier lines.

What we can integrate

If it has an interface, it can be connected.

Three routes, depending on what the system offers.

01

Public APIs

Full integration: read, write, events and notifications, with the service's official credentials.

02

Web interfaces without public APIs

If a service is used from a browser, we automate the same calls its web panel makes, within the service's terms of use.

03

Serial and Modbus

For devices without a network or with industrial protocols, a small bridge program reads the serial or Modbus port and delivers data to your systems.

FAQ

Answers before you ask.

Can I keep my numbers?

Yes, by porting them to a VoIP carrier.

Do I need a special line?

A stable Internet connection is enough; with many simultaneous calls we check bandwidth.

Can the intercom ring on my mobile?

Yes, with SIP intercoms connected to the phone system: answer and open from anywhere.

Do you install the cabling?

Wiring, lines and electrical panels are installed by licensed electricians under Italian law (DM 37/08), whom we work with. We take care of devices, configuration, software and data.
