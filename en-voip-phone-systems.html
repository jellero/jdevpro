FAQ

Answers before you ask.

Can I keep my numbers?

Yes, by porting them to a VoIP carrier.

Do I need a special line?

A stable Internet connection is enough; with many simultaneous calls we check bandwidth.

Can the intercom ring on my mobile?

Yes, with SIP intercoms connected to the phone system: answer and open from anywhere.

Do you record calls?

Only where needed and in line with privacy rules, informing callers.

Do you install the cabling?

Wiring, lines and electrical panels are installed by licensed electricians under Italian law (DM 37/08), whom we work with. We take care of devices, configuration, software and data.
