FAQ

Answers before you ask.

Can I start from Excel?

Yes. We import the data you already have, with preview and duplicate checks.

Does it replace my accounting software?

No. Accounting and e-invoicing stay in your program; the new software works alongside it and passes data to it.

How much does it cost?

Phased work at a fixed price agreed before starting, plus a monthly plan for hosting and support.

Where does the software run?

On a cloud server or on a server at your company, depending on where the data must stay.

Who maintains it after delivery?

We do, with a monthly plan covering backups, security updates, fixes and small developments.
