What we can integrate

If it has an interface, it can be connected.

Four routes, depending on what the system offers.

01

Public APIs

Full integration: read, write, events and notifications, with the service's official credentials.

02

Web interfaces without public APIs

If a service is used from a browser, we automate the same calls its web panel makes, within the service's terms of use.

03

Serial and Modbus

For devices without a network or with industrial protocols, a small bridge program reads the serial or Modbus port and delivers data to your systems.

04

Added sensors

For machines and plants with no interface at all, old ones included: sensors and meters detect status, cycles, times and consumption and bring them into your systems.

FAQ

Answers before you ask.

Does it work without Internet?

Yes. Data is stored on site and sent when the connection is back.

Can I use the sensors I already have?

Often yes. We check protocols and interfaces before proposing new devices.

Which brands can be integrated?

All those with public APIs, such as Tuya, Aqara, Honeywell, Shelly, Netatmo, Philips Hue, Fronius, SMA or SolarEdge. If a device only has an app or web panel, we automate its calls; if it only has a serial or Modbus port, we use a bridge program.

Can you size the power supply?

Yes. We size panels and batteries on real consumption even when they only need to power a sensor, a router or a camera. Electrical work is done by licensed installers.

Is the data secure?

Communication is encrypted and devices sit on networks separated from the office network.

Do you do the wiring?

Wiring, lines and electrical panels are installed by licensed electricians under Italian law (DM 37/08), whom we work with. We take care of devices, configuration, software and data.
