JDEVPRO · Software and systems integration for businesses

JDEVPRO: software and integration for businesses. Industry 4.0 connections, from PLCs to business software.

We design software, integrations and connected systems for businesses. In manufacturing, we connect machines to the management software already in use, building integrations and adaptations where technically feasible rather than requiring a new ERP. Where no suitable application is available, we can provide a simple dedicated management tool. We manage review, development, testing and documentation in north-east Italy and on site where needed.

Interconnection and requirements

Documented technical work, independent tax assessment.

Interconnection is one of the requirements to assess for eligible investments. JDEVPRO delivers and documents the technical integration; tax eligibility must be evaluated by the appointed specialists.
