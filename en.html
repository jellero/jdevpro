Why JDEVPRO

An electrician installs. A programmer codes. We hold the whole system together.

When machines, installations, network and software have to work together, someone has to govern the whole instead of many separate suppliers. We design, connect and document the system; wiring and panels are done by licensed installers we work with.

Partners: Efficasa for solar, DWIN for HMI displays, Aruba Business for hosting, Siportal for VoIP.
Technologies and services we use in projects: Open Fiber for FTTH fibre, Aruba for connectivity and cloud, MikroTik and Ubiquiti for networks, connectivity and video surveillance, WuBook for OTA channels.
