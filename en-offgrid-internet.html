The problem

Sound familiar?

  • No fixed line. No fibre or ADSL, and the phone signal is weak.
  • No power. The site has no mains power or only intermittently.
  • Every problem means a trip. A frozen router is enough to drive all the way up.

FAQ

Answers before you ask.

How much does the connection cost?

It depends on the type: a data SIM or a satellite plan. We help you choose a plan that matches real usage.

Have you worked on isolated sites?

Yes. We have built off-grid systems, Ubiquiti and MikroTik radio links and Starlink connections: we size power and connectivity on real consumption, small systems included.

Does it work in winter?

We size panels and batteries for the months with least sun and set up alerts when the battery drops.

Do you install the electrical system?

Wiring, lines and electrical panels are installed by licensed electricians under Italian law (DM 37/08), whom we work with. We take care of devices, configuration, software and data.
