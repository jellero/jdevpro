SmartDrive

Operational security, not simply cloud storage.

A standalone product designed to protect business documents with incremental backup, version history, recovery and easy multi-device management. SmartAgent integration is optional. Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone are all planned at launch.

Standalone

Incremental folder backup, version history and recovery without a SmartAgent subscription.

With SmartAgent

Optionally link files to clients and cases without making CRM mandatory.

Multi-device

Manage devices in one place, with Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone apps in the planned launch product.

Windows · Mac

Incremental backup for business documents on Windows and Mac.

SmartDrive is designed for incremental backup of work folders, recoverable file versions and restore. Its interface will simplify management of connected devices. Windows distribution is planned through Microsoft Store and Mac is part of the same complete product. Recovery must pass testing before release.

Simple, secure pairing.

The planned pairing process uses a single-use code valid for 2 minutes with a countdown. Each device receives its own revocable hidden credential. No server address or account password is entered into the app.

Android · iPhone

Android and iPhone photo, video and data backup.

The Android and iPhone apps are part of the planned SmartDrive launch product, not a separate later offering. They will back up photos, videos, files and accessible user-authorised data, within Android and iOS permissions and restrictions.

  • Photo and video backup
  • Selected accessible files and data
  • Access and recovery across devices

NanoDrive · SmartDrive

NanoDrive: keep your data on your own hardware.

Keep documents, photos and files on your own devices while accessing them securely from outside the office. NanoDrive is our planned hardware for connecting NAS units, external hard drives and USB flash drives for remote file access, without requiring cloud storage.

€50

Hardware + first 12 months of service

From month 13: planned renewal at €2/month

Planned launch offer: NanoDrive hardware for €50 with the first 12 months of remote-access service included. Planned renewal from month 13: €2/month. VAT and shipping terms to be confirmed. Italy-based access infrastructure is planned but not yet verified.

Keeping files locally does not create an off-site backup. A separate copy is necessary to protect against hardware failure, theft or site incidents.

NanoDrive — technical specifications

  • Quad-core Rockchip RK3528A
  • 1 GB or 2 GB LPDDR4X RAM
  • Gigabit Ethernet
  • USB 3.0 and USB-C
  • Optional eMMC module support
  • Planned connection to NAS devices, HDDs and USB drives
