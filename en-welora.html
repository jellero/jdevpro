Product · Hospitality management software

Welora: all your property software for €29 a month.

Welora brings PMS, booking engine, OTA channels, digital check-in, payments and POS, invoices, housekeeping, till and automation into one system, on the same data. For B&Bs, guest houses, hotels and booking-based businesses.

Welora website ↗Let's talk

The problem

You don't have too few rooms: you have too many tools.

Costs that add up

PMS, booking engine, payments, compliance, invoices and automation sold as separate products or add-ons.

Data copied by hand

The same booking travels through portals, spreadsheets, messages and different tools.

Automation only for the big ones

Useful features come only with expensive plans, when they are needed from day one.

What's included

A complete base, not a catalogue of add-ons.

PMS, planning and bookings

Rooms, units, availability, rates, restrictions and daily operations.

Booking engine and channels

Direct sales with no Welora commission and synchronised OTA channels.

Check-in and compliance

Guest data and documents collected online, Italian police and statistics reporting prepared.

Payments and phone POS

Checkout, pre-authorisations, payments and Tap to Pay on compatible devices.

Services and extras

Breakfasts, transfers, parking, rentals or treatments, even without a room.

Receipts and invoices

Italian fiscal documents and e-invoicing built in.

Operational automation

Rules triggering messages, housekeeping, access and payments, with history.

Multiple properties and roles

Separate data per property and a central view when needed.

Operations

What usually costs another tool is already inside.

  • Guided import
  • Reception and shifts
  • Housekeeping on the phone
  • Till and ledger
  • Smart building and locks

Switch to Welora without losing data: contacts, bookings, rooms, rates and open balances are imported from CSV or Excel, with a preview you can fix line by line.

Reception gets a prioritised daily view, guided checkout, key log and shift handovers. Housekeeping arrives on staff phones with checklists. Till and ledger are connected, with exports for the accountant.

Price

€29 a month, all included.

  • All features included
  • No commission on direct bookings
  • Switch without losing data

No modules to unlock and no higher plans. You only pay extra for real costs: €10 a month for each OTA channel beyond the first.

Prices exclude VAT. External provider fees (payments, identity checks, portals) are separate. During pre-launch you can lock a 50% discount for 12 months on welora.it.

Status

Pre-launch, expected on 15 February 2027.

  • Platform with 25 modules, from bookings to the till
  • Separate automation engine that loses no data when a service is down
  • Connections with Booking.com, Stripe, Italian police and statistics portals, e-invoicing, WhatsApp and thermostats
  • First pilot with properties in Friuli Venezia Giulia

Some features depend on external providers and on the mode chosen by the property.
