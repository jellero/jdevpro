FAQ

Answers before you ask.

Does it work with my existing property?

Yes. We start from existing locks, thermostats and programs and add only what is missing.

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Codes and instructions arrive by email or message.

What if the Internet goes down?

The locks and thermostats we choose also work offline; data syncs when the connection is back.

How is this different from professional building automation?

Here we connect the guest journey: booking, online check-in, access codes and compliance. Building automation covers the property's installation: climate, lights, sensors and energy, designed to last. They are often done together.

Do you do the wiring?

Wiring, lines and electrical panels are installed by licensed electricians under Italian law (DM 37/08), whom we work with. We take care of devices, configuration, software and data.
