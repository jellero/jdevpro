Digital Receptionist: the AI receptionist that answers and works with your systems.

Digital Receptionist answers calls in natural Italian, understands the caller's request and turns it into a useful action or structured record. It connects with VoIP phone systems and business software, routes calls, handles bookings and cancellations and notifies staff. It connects phone calls with authorised business workflows.

What it does

Answers in natural Italian

Converses without keypad menus and always identifies itself as a virtual assistant.

Direct answering, screening or backup

Answers calls, routes callers to teams or steps in when lines are busy, after hours or unanswered.

Connects with existing VoIP

SIP/PBX connectors for business telephony, with configuration and compatibility checks for each phone system.

SmartAgent, Welora and other platforms

Integrates with CRM, calendars and management systems to identify customers, read information and write authorised updates.

Bookings, cancellations and rescheduling

Uses real availability and business rules rather than inventing confirmations or permissions.

Structured call records

Captures caller details, confirmed contact information, reason, priority and missing details.

Staff notifications

Sends configurable alerts and summaries when staff action is needed.

Approved knowledge and safeguards

Answers using approved services, hours and procedures; business software validates operations.

Integrations

Configuration and integration

Digital Receptionist is configured according to the business phone system, workflows and management software. Compatibility is assessed for each integration.

The AI understands. The software validates.

Digital Receptionist does not invent prices, availability or response times. It uses approved information, follows business rules and always identifies itself as a virtual assistant.
