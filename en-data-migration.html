Data migration · Business software and databases

Data migration: change software, lose nothing.

We move your data from the old business software, a database or Excel into the new system: master data, items, bills of materials, stock, documents and balances. First we clean it, then we test it with you, and finally we hand over a comparison of source and target totals.

Let's talk See real examples

The problem

Sound familiar?

  • The new software is ready, the data is in the old one. And the old supplier offers no complete export, or charges a lot for it.
  • Standard imports leave half the data behind. Whatever does not fit the expected CSV is lost or lands in the wrong fields.
  • Years of dirty data. Duplicate customers, wrong tax codes, phone numbers in the name field, items never used.
  • Nobody knows whether the numbers match. After the switch, stock and balances do not match, and it shows months later.

What you get

What we do, in practice.

Extraction from any source

From the old software's database, Access, SQL Server, MySQL, Firebird or DBF, from exports and Excel.

Clean-up and normalisation

Duplicates merged, tax and VAT codes checked, addresses and phones in the right fields, codes made consistent.

Mapping to the new system

Every source field has its destination, documented and approved by you.

Trial migration

A first import into a test environment, to check calmly before the real switch.

Reconciliation report

Source and target counts and totals compared: master data, stock, balances, documents.

Originals kept safe

The old database stays untouched and you get a full copy in an open format.

FAQ

Answers before you ask.

The old software has no export: can it still be done?

Almost always. If the program stores data in a database or readable files, we read them directly without changing the old system. Before quoting we check what can be read.

Do we have to stop working?

No. Trials and checks run in parallel while you keep using the old program; the final switch usually takes half a day, on an agreed date.

How do we know all the data arrived?

With the reconciliation report: counts of master data, items and documents, stock and balances compared between source and target.

Do you migrate historical documents?

Yes, when the new system can hold them. Otherwise we archive them in a readable format so the history stays available.

What about privacy?

We handle the data as appointed data processor, on protected copies, and delete working copies at the end.
