Where it runs

In the cloud or on your premises: your choice.

The software is the same in all three cases and runs in the browser on PCs, tablets and phones. What changes is where the data lives and who looks after the machine.

Managed cloud

Dedicated server in a European data centre; we handle updates, backups and monitoring. For companies without in-house IT or with several sites.

Company server

On your own physical or virtual server. Keeps working if the internet goes down; remote access via VPN or Cloudflare Tunnel.

Company NAS

In containers on suitable Synology or QNAP NAS units, with disk and cloud backups. Suitable for up to about 15 concurrent users.

Security

Production data is worth as much as the machines.

  • Personal users and two-step verification
  • Permissions by department
  • Change history
  • VPN or Cloudflare Tunnel
  • Encrypted off-site backups

Every person has their own user, with two-step verification for remote access. Roles separate departments: operators see their operations, not prices or accounts. Every change to a document stays in its history, with author and date.

Servers and NAS units expose no ports to the internet: remote access goes through a VPN or a Cloudflare Tunnel with company login. Backups are daily, encrypted and stored off-site, with periodic restore tests. In the cloud, data stays in the European Union under a data processing agreement.
